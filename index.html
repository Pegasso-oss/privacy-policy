Privacy Policy — Smash the Boss

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Smash the Boss is developed by PegassoDev. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Data We Collect

Smash the Boss does not collect any personal information directly. However, the app uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — used to process in-app purchases. Purchase data is handled by Google Play. See Google Play's Privacy Policy.

Local Storage

The app saves your game progress (wave, upgrades, achievements) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. You may see personalized ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases processed by Google Play. We do not store or have access to your payment information.

Children

Smash the Boss is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
